Patient Assistance & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct clinical answers about hospital admissions, maternity care, accepted HMOs, and emergency protocols in Port Harcourt.

Where is Fimforte Specialist Hospital located in Port Harcourt?

Fimforte Specialist Hospital Ltd is centrally situated at Number 1 Bloombreed School Road, Opposite NTA, Mgbuoba, Port Harcourt 500272, Rivers State, Nigeria. The medical center is conveniently accessible from NTA Road, Choba, University of Port Harcourt, and Ada George Road.

Is Fimforte Specialist Hospital open 24 hours for emergency admissions?

Yes. Fimforte operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Our casualty officers, specialist obstetricians, surgeons, and nurses are always on active duty for medical emergencies, trauma admissions, and labor & maternity deliveries.

Which HMOs and health insurance schemes are accepted at Fimforte?

We are a certified NHIS / NHIA healthcare provider and accept 22+ accredited private HMOs, state contributory schemes, and corporate retainerships. Supported partners include Reliance HMO, Hygeia HMO, IHMS, HCI Healthcare, Hyssop, Leadway Health, Bastion, Anchor, Springtide, Synergy, MB&O, Oceanic Health, Dot HMO, United Health, Mediplan, NEM Health, Avon HMO, Rivers State Contributory Health (RIVCHPP), Clearline, Health Assur, Alleanza, Medical Partners, and major energy corporate retainers. If your scheme is not listed, our front desk can immediately liaise with your provider.

How can I book an appointment with Dr. Fimber Chukwuka or other specialists?

You can schedule an appointment online using the Book Appointment button on this website, connect directly with our 24/7 medical triage desk on WhatsApp at +234 701 635 7096, or call +234 803 931 7663.

What maternity and gynaecological services are available?

Supervised by Lead Specialist Dr. Fimber Chukwuka (Consultant Obstetrician & Gynaecologist), our clinic offers comprehensive antenatal care (ANC), ultrasound diagnostics, safe deliveries, elective & emergency Caesarean sections (C-sections), fertility evaluations, and gynaecological surgery in sterile suites.

How quickly are laboratory and diagnostic results processed?

Our in-house clinical diagnostic laboratory provides automated hematology, clinical chemistry, and infectious screening with fast turnarounds. Routine blood tests, pregnancy panels, and malaria assays are typically delivered in under 24 hours.
